A blood coordination desk, built around verified records

LifeLink is a donor coordination platform for blood requests, emergency needs and donation camps. It keeps the three sides of a donation in one place: the patient's request, the donor's availability, and the record an administrator verifies afterwards.

For donors

See only what you can help with

A donor's dashboard lists the open requests their blood group can serve, the reason each one matches, the 90-day eligibility countdown and the camps nearby. Availability can be switched off at any time.

For recipients

Follow a request from raised to fulfilled

Each request carries a status timeline, the donor responses, and the compatible donor pool with the number of units still needed. Emergency requests are separated into their own queue.

For administrators

Verify before anything counts

Administrators verify donation records, issue certificates, confirm volunteer duties and generate camp check-in. Analytics and CSV exports come from the same database.

The path of a request

Every status change is written to the request history, so the timeline on the request page is the real audit trail.

What the platform will not do

It will not approve a transfusion

Compatibility shown here is red-cell guidance for finding donors. Crossmatching and medical screening at the hospital decide.

It will not publish phone numbers

Donor contact details stay hidden unless the donor publishes them or responds to a specific request.

It will not count an unverified donation

Donation statistics, achievements and certificates are produced only from records an administrator has verified.

{{ disclaimer }}

Volunteering

Camps run on volunteers. Duties cover the registration desk, guiding donors, refreshments, logistics and the awareness stall. An administrator confirms each duty before the camp, and confirmed duties count towards achievements.
